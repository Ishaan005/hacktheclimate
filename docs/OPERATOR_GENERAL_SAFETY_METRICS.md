# General safety metrics for operator action combinations

**Working draft, 29 September 2026.** This is the **family-level safety layer** for the three families and situations in the [locked scope](OPERATOR_SCENARIO_SCOPE.md). It applies to any proposed *combination* of actions. The later action-specific layer will ask whether each named asset, switch, customer or counterparty can deliver its part. A safety result is a **pass, fail or unknown gate**; renewable energy, cost or carbon cannot compensate for a failed or unverified gate. EirGrid/SONI distinguish thermal security, dynamic stability, minimum conventional units, reserves and ramping, while noting that these requirements interact. [Operational Policy Roadmap 2025–2035](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf).

## What every suggestion must show

- **Baseline and time window:** the current operating plan, including instructions already in force; continuing that plan with no new instruction; the candidate combination; and an operator-entered alternative, all over the same time window. EirGrid/SONI continually reassess indicative schedules and actual instructions. [Balancing Market Principles Statement (BMPS) v9, §§4.3–4.5](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Balancing_Market_Principles_Statement_Version_9.0.pdf).
- **Combined result:** add the actions at their actual electrical locations and delivery times, then rerun the relevant security studies. Do not add independent “MW relief” estimates as if they could not interact. Show the worst result across the interval and required credible failures. This is **our proposed comparison method**, based on the operators' normal and contingency security duties. [EirGrid Operating Security Standards, April 2025](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Operating-Security-Standards-Version-3.0-April-2025.pdf).
- **Current rule and evidence:** identify the rating or operating rule effective at the decision time, input timestamp, forecast issue time and study version. Mark a missing or stale safety result **unknown**, not safe. Show other active family limits alongside the primary family metric. This display convention is **our proposal**; the operator must confirm the exact acceptance rules. [Operational Policy Roadmap, policy review process](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf).

## Transmission Constraint

**Primary question:** Does the named line, cable or transformer, and every route affected by the combined actions, stay within its applicable thermal rating? For the locked scope, an intact-network risk includes one credible failure; an outage risk includes the existing outage and one further credible failure. Transmission-line voltage is outside this scenario catalogue. EirGrid's operating standards require transmission elements to respect rated current, including the rules for temporary admissible overload. [Locked scope](OPERATOR_SCENARIO_SCOPE.md); [Operating Security Standards, §5](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Operating-Security-Standards-Version-3.0-April-2025.pdf).

| General safety metric | Calculation and pass question | Why the operator cares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Current and forecast thermal margin** | Applicable rating minus measured or studied loading, for every monitored line, cable and transformer, in the unit used by the TSO tool (A, MVA or percent of rating). Use the rating valid for that asset, topology and duration. Does every margin pass throughout the risk window? | Shows whether the existing overload clears and stays clear. |
| **Worst credible-failure margin** | Minimum margin over the required contingency set **after the full combination**: one failure with an intact network, or one further failure during an outage. Does the worst case pass? | Prevents a safe present flow from hiding a post-trip overload. |
| **New bottleneck and time to relief** | Minimum margin on all affected routes, plus time when the binding route would breach its rating versus time when relief is delivered. Does relief arrive first without moving the overload? | Ensures that a different line is not sacrificed and that action is timely. |

**Data needed:** live network topology and flows, equipment ratings, outage state, current instructions and a validated power-flow/contingency model. The project has a [planning network sensitivity](NETWORK_SAFETY_ACTIONS.md), not a live security pass. Balance, reserve, stability and SNSP remain cross-checks when an action changes them. [EirGrid Wind Dispatch Tool constraint groups](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Wind-Dispatch-Tool-Constraint-Group-Overview_1.pdf).

## High Frequency / Minimum Generation

**Primary question:** Which of the four tracked requirements is actually limiting, and does the combined action satisfy **every** requirement that binds? Line loading is not the primary proof for these system-wide cases, although a dispatch change still must pass any affected network limit. EirGrid's public High Frequency / Minimum Generation reason combines the causes, so its MWh cannot identify which one applied. [Locked scope](OPERATOR_SCENARIO_SCOPE.md); [2025 dispatch-down report](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Annual-Renewable-Constraint-and-Curtailment-Report-2025-V1.0.pdf).

| Limiting requirement | General safety metric and calculation | Pass question and operator use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Measured high frequency** | Live frequency (Hz), deviation from 50 Hz, trend, and expected deliverable MW response, followed by observed delivery; forecast recovery after the full combination. Use high-rate measurements, not half-hour averages. | Does response remove the surplus quickly enough and return frequency to the effective operating range? EirGrid/SONI use actual frequency deviation to act promptly. [LFC Block Operational Agreement, December 2024, Art. 5](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/LFCBOA%20-Load-Frequency-Control-Block-Operational-Agreement-for-Ireland-and-Northern-Ireland.pdf). |
| **Minimum conventional generation** | Qualified units remaining online in **Ireland and Northern Ireland**, each jurisdiction's effective required count, and each unit's technical minimum MW. Track the full transition, not only the end state. | Are both applicable counts met at every step while minimum output, balance and required support remain feasible? Inertia is a separate concurrent gate, not a substitute for unit count. [Operational Policy Roadmap, dynamic stability](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf). |
| **Reserve** | Qualified, *deliverable* reserve MW minus required MW, by direction, response tranche and applicable jurisdiction. Include response time, sustain time and energy; avoid double-counting the same MW. | Does the effective reserve requirement still have coverage after the combination? The 2024 agreement dimensions positive and negative frequency-restoration reserve and notes geographical limits. [LFC Block Operational Agreement, December 2024, Art. 4](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/LFCBOA%20-Load-Frequency-Control-Block-Operational-Agreement-for-Ireland-and-Northern-Ireland.pdf). |
| **Ramping** | Deliverable ramp MW minus required ramp MW at each applicable future deadline, in the binding direction. Use the dated net-load trajectory, start notice, MW/minute and sustainable energy. | Can the system follow the coming change? Published RM1, RM3 and RM8 define upward delivery within 1, 3 and 8 hours; confirm the effective downward rule before applying a downward requirement. [EirGrid/SONI ramping-margin note](https://www.sem-o.com/sites/semo/files/documents/general-publications/Ramping_Margin_Requirements_in_Scheduling.pdf). |

**Data needed:** live frequency and response, effective rules, unit status/minima, qualified service awards, forecasts and time-stamped instructions. Also check all-island inertia and dynamic stability if the combination changes synchronous machines or fast response. The project's half-hour context and combined reason label cannot certify these results. [Operational Policy Roadmap](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf); [data guide](DATA_GUIDE.md).

## SNSP

**Primary question:** Does the **all-island non-synchronous share** remain within the limit effective for the interval, without violating a separate dynamic-stability requirement? Rising renewable output, falling demand, rising imports and falling exports are four ways to reach this **same** case. EirGrid/SONI define SNSP using non-synchronous generation and net HVDC imports relative to demand and net HVDC exports; the treatment of batteries and transfers must follow the effective policy. [Locked scope](OPERATOR_SCENARIO_SCOPE.md); [Operational Policy Roadmap, SNSP definition](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf).

| General safety metric | Calculation and pass question | Why the operator cares |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SNSP margin** | Recalculate the ratio from current/forecast wind, solar, demand and **signed** HVDC imports/exports under the effective definition. Show limit minus candidate SNSP in **percentage points** at each time step. Is the worst margin safe? | Shows real ratio headroom; extra consumption cannot automatically be credited if extra non-synchronous output is admitted at the same time. |
| **Inertia and frequency-stability margin** | Qualified all-island inertia minus its effective floor, plus the required studied rate-of-change-of-frequency and other dynamic-security results for the candidate state. Do all applicable gates pass? | A safe SNSP ratio alone does not prove the system will withstand a disturbance. [Operational Policy Roadmap, dynamic stability](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/EirGrid-SONI-Operational-Policy-Roadmap-2025-2035.pdf). |
| **Duration and concurrent limits** | Worst ratio and stability result during action ramp, storage rebound and the forecast window; check co-binding unit, reserve and transmission limits. Does the combined plan remain safe throughout? | Prevents a momentary SNSP improvement from creating a later breach. This time-path display is **our proposed operator view**. [BMPS v9, §§4.3–4.5](https://cms.eirgrid.ie/sites/default/files/publications/Balancing_Market_Principles_Statement_Version_9.0.pdf). |

**Data needed:** effective limit, correctly signed live and forecast components, current instructions, validated battery treatment and TSO dynamic-security results. The project has [historical half-hour SNSP and component measurements](EIRGRID_SOURCES.md) for context or a what-if calculation from supplied inputs, but no live safety pass. [Data feasibility audit](NETWORK_DATA_FEASIBILITY_2026-09-28.md).

## What remains for the action-specific layer

For each action in the [candidate list](OPERATOR_ACTIONS_AND_METRICS.md), use the [action-specific safety gates](OPERATOR_ACTION_SPECIFIC_SAFETY_METRICS.md) to add its named asset or party, instruction authority, location, present availability, response time, energy duration, permission and side effects. Then rerun **all** applicable family gates for the **whole combination**. Only after they pass should the operator compare avoided and remaining dispatch-down MWh, net resource cost or carbon. The exact thresholds, contingency set and on-screen ordering need operator confirmation; this document does not set new operating policy.
